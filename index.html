Open this on your Mac 💻

Luchi is a Mac app for now. Open this page on your Mac to download it.

Setting up (about 2 minutes)

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Luchi into Applications.
  2. Open Luchi. If your Mac says it “can't be opened”, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway (only needed once).
  3. Luchi asks which brain to use. Pick ChatGPT and sign in, or Claude if you have Claude Code.
  4. Click Allow for the microphone, press ⌥ Space (Option + Space) and say hi!

Good to know

Everything Luchi remembers stays on your Mac. It uses your own ChatGPT or Claude plan. When a new version comes out, the fox will tell you.
